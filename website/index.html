Quick Launch for Windows 10 & 11

Features

Built for quick-launch speed,
not clutter.

Pin in Seconds

Add files and folders instantly, plus Start menu apps from the Window Apps picker.

Acrylic, If You Want It

Keep the launcher lightweight and clean, with optional Windows acrylic blur and adjustable opacity.

Tray-First Launcher

DockTray lives in the system tray so your shortcuts are always close, never in the way.

Shortcut Controls

Fine-tune icon size, button size, tray size, themes, and position so launching stays fast and familiar.

Clean Themes

Dark, Light, Acrylic Dark, and Acrylic Light, with adjustable acrylic opacity and live theme updates.

Taskbar Aware

Choose bottom, top, left, or right taskbar layouts in settings to keep the launcher within easy reach.

Drag Mode

Toggle Drag Mode to drop files, folders, and apps safely when you want, without accidental drops.
